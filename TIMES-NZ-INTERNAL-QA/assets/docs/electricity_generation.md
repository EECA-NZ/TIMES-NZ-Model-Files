This page covers electricity generation, including generation output, fuels used for generation, and generation capacity. Battery capacity is shown separately.

You can also view electricity generation by timeslice, although this can only be shown for one year at a time.

TIMES-NZ 3.0 models energy supply and demand across different timeslices, or parts of the year. These are:

- 4 seasons: spring, summer, autumn, and winter
- 2 day types: weekday and weekend
- 3 times of day: day, night, and peak. Peak refers to the hour between 6 and 7 pm.
