This page covers final energy demand.

It excludes energy used in transformation processes such as electricity generation, and non-energy use of fuels such as natural gas used as feedstock.

You can also view electricity demand by timeslice, although this can only be shown for one year at a time.

TIMES-NZ 3.0 models energy supply and demand across different timeslices, or parts of the year. These are:

- 4 seasons: spring, summer, autumn, and winter
- 2 day types: weekday and weekend
- 3 times of day: day, night, and peak. Peak refers to the hour between 6 and 7 pm.
