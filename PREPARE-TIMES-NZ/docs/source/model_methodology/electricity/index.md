# Electricity

This section provides a comprehensive overview of the methodologies, assumptions, and technical parameters used to model the electricity supply sector in TIMES-NZ 3.0. 

The document includes: 
 - A brief overview of the TIMES optimisation method,
 - The modelling of the base year for electricity supply,
 - Technical parameters and learning curve adjustments of potential future electricity generation technologies,
 - Transmission and distribution technical parameters and costs, including the HVDC,
 - Peak demand and margin constraints, 
 - Emissions factors, 
 - Battery storage assumptions
