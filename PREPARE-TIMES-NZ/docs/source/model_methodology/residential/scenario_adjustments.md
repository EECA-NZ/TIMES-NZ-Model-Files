# Scenario adjustments


Currently, no adjustments are made to residential demand profiles, exogenous demand projections, or technology parameters between the TIMES-NZ Traditional or Transformation scenarios. 

However, changes in upstream energy supply between scenarios, such as electricity generation or battery technology costs, may impact the most cost-effective technology composition in residential energy demand. 

Many other energy scenario models include changes to population projections, and therefore residential energy demand, between different scenarios. While this is a useful approach, TIMES-NZ is focused on the impact of different energy or policy options on the energy system while keeping demand fixed. 

There is capacity to adjust or test different proposed sensitivities to this sector according to user needs.
