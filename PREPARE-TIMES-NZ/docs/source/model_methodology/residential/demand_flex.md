# Demand flex

For the residential sector, we currently enable demand flex options for hot water heating. This allows the model to shift hot water demand across different time periods within a day. There is a cost associated with this shifting in the form of heat loss, 

This method is currently enabled for electric hot water cylinders. We assume that a maximum of **50%** of total hot water cylinder demand can be shifted from any given timeslice, moving it from peak times to day or night load. We assume that **1%** of heat is lost in this process. In the Transformation scenario, the maximum share increases linearly to **90%** by 2050, reflecting increased use of smart hot water heating over time. 

This method is scalable to other demand flex options, such as Home Energy Management Systems.
