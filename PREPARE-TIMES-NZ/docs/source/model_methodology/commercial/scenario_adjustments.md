# Overview 

TIMES-NZ produces a model solution that meets projected commercial energy demand while minimising costs. The model uses detailed costs and technical parameters of: 
 - Existing commercial demand
 - Demand projections
 - Existing technology parameters
 - Future technologies

## Scenario adjustments 

Commercial demand parameters are varied between scenarios due to differences in data centre demand assumptions, with the Transformation scenario assuming a higher rate of growth and efficiency increases. More details are provided in [Demand Projections](demand_projections.md).
